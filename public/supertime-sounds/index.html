supertime sounds

Eight short notification candidates for the supertime iOS app, shaped around daily ritual, polished puzzle completion, warm paper/ink texture, and the ruby brand accent.

01 daily ruby tick0.82s

Daily Ruby Tick

Flagship candidate

Crisp clock tick into a warm two-note ruby chime. Precise, polished, and recognizably supertime.

02 paper lantern0.94s

Paper Lantern

Soft daily ritual

Paper-like tap with a warm, restrained bell tail. Best for frequent notifications.

03 wordie win0.92s

Wordie Win

Puzzle-complete energy

Three tight rising plucks with a small sparkle, tuned for an accomplished daily-game feeling.

04 tomorrow gate1.12s

Tomorrow Gate

Curious return cue

Soft movement into an open bell. More reflective and less task-like.

05 focus nudge0.58s

Focus Nudge

Most restrained

A minimal tick-pluck pair. Designed to be audible without becoming part of the room.

06 group spark0.74s

Group Spark

Social notification

Syncopated pair of pings that feels social without copying chat-app sounds.

07 super lift1.00s

Super Lift

Most elevated

Tiny lift into a bright chime. Stronger brand color, useful if the app needs a more memorable hook.

08 endgame stamp0.90s

Endgame Stamp

Completion/streak

Confident tap and open chime. More decisive than the flagship, still not alarm-like.
