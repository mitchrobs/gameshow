research-backed batch 2

supertime sounds

Twenty new custom notification candidates designed as a coherent sound family: short enough for iOS, distinctive enough to learn, pleasant enough to repeat, and grounded in supertime's daily puzzle ritual.

Memorable: repeated short-short-long motifs, not random pings.
Useful: clear onset and mid-frequency energy for phone speakers.
Kind: low roughness, controlled brightness, and short tails.
Branded: paper, tile, ruby, time, and solved-puzzle cues.
