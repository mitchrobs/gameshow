# supertime simple sound candidates

Ten minimal variants based on the direction of v2 candidate 19, Ink Ping.

The design constraint is one short gesture: a tactile onset, a clean tonal body, and a controlled tail.

Candidates:
- `30_simple_ink_ping`: Simple Ink Ping - Closest to 19. Keeps the core appeal of 19 while making the onset a little cleaner.
- `31_soft_ink_ping`: Soft Ink Ping - Gentler daily cue. Better if 19 feels a touch too shiny after repeated listens.
- `32_bright_ink_ping`: Bright Ink Ping - Most audible. Cuts through phone speakers without becoming a full alert.
- `33_low_ink_ping`: Low Ink Ping - Warmest simple cue. More mature and less sparkly, but still very short.
- `34_paper_dot`: Paper Dot - Most tactile. A simple cue with direct supertime material texture.
- `35_tile_dot`: Tile Dot - Shortest physical cue. Great if the sound should feel more object-like than musical.
- `36_glass_dot`: Glass Dot - Cleanest cue. Minimal and premium, with the least obvious clock/game metaphor.
- `37_ruby_dot`: Ruby Dot - Warm bright balance. A good middle path between 30, 31, and 32.
- `38_focus_dot`: Focus Dot - Quietest practical cue. Probably the safest option for frequent notifications.
- `39_final_dot`: Final Dot - Strong simple candidate. The most finished-feeling simple candidate in this mini batch.
