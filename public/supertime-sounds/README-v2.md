# supertime notification sounds v2

Twenty research-informed candidates generated as a coherent sound family.

Design rubric:
- Short motifs beat one-off pings for recall.
- Auditory-icon textures make abstract notifications feel connected to the product.
- Low roughness, moderate brightness, and short tails reduce fatigue.
- Enough transient and 700-2500 Hz content helps playback on phone speakers.
- Variants share a motif family so the brand can adapt across daily, social, and completion states.

Files:
- `wav/`: 48 kHz stereo 16-bit WAV masters.
- `caf/`: iOS-friendly CAF/IMA4 exports.
- `m4a/`: AAC web audition exports for Safari/iPhone.

Candidates:
- `09_time_signature`: Time Signature - Lead candidate. The clearest brand mnemonic: fast, elegant, and easy to recognize after a few repetitions.
- `10_ruby_celeste`: Ruby Celeste - Premium daily cue. Feels more crafted and less app-generic while staying gentle enough for repeated use.
- `11_paper_tile`: Paper Tile - Puzzle-world cue. Connects the sound to supertime's paper/ink puzzle surfaces without literal nostalgia.
- `12_dawn_resolve`: Dawn Resolve - Soft morning return. Good if the daily notification should feel inviting rather than task-like.
- `13_solved_stamp`: Solved Stamp - Completion cue. Confident enough for wins and streaks without becoming an alarm.
- `14_group_glint`: Group Glint - Social cue. Reads as social activity while avoiding common chat-app cadences.
- `15_tomorrow_bell`: Tomorrow Bell - Reflective return cue. Useful for a gentle 'new puzzle is waiting' moment.
- `16_focus_margin`: Focus Margin - Most restrained. Best for people who dislike prominent custom notification sounds.
- `17_daily_arc`: Daily Arc - Elegant lift. Has motion and optimism, but less sparkle than a game-completion cue.
- `18_morning_check`: Morning Check - Routine cue. Feels like a habit marker rather than a message alert.
- `19_ink_ping`: Ink Ping - Shortest bright cue. A strong option if the sound must be extremely short but identifiable.
- `20_warm_win`: Warm Win - Friendly success cue. More playful than the lead candidate but still polished.
- `21_thread_reply`: Thread Reply - Group response cue. Useful for later social features where a reply should sound distinct.
- `22_streak_seal`: Streak Seal - Reward cue. A little more ceremonial, best for solved/streak states.
- `23_invite_spark`: Invite Spark - New social event. Feels like something arrived from another person, not a timer.
- `24_calendar_lift`: Calendar Lift - Daily availability. The most literal 'time' cue in the set.
- `25_glass_route`: Glass Route - Premium motion cue. Most 'app-like' in the positive sense: clean, modern, and kinetic.
- `26_polite_alert`: Polite Alert - Stronger reminder. For a reminder that needs to cut through, but not feel like a warning.
- `27_game_room`: Game Room - Playful puzzle cue. More playful and physical, good if the brand should lean game-first.
- `28_ruby_finish`: Ruby Finish - Alternate flagship. The most memorable alternate if the lead candidate feels too understated.
